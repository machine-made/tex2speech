The flow obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ns}
\frac{\partial \vel{u}}{\partial t} + (\vel{u}\cdot\nabla)\vel{u} = -\nabla p + \nu \nabla^2 \vel{u}
\end{equation}
with $\nabla \cdot \vel{u} = 0$.
